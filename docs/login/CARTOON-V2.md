Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background illustration for Spirit Gate, a 2D pixel-character fantasy RPG login screen. Create a single wide landscape 16:9 illustration, no interface. Art direction: distinctly CARTOON 2D game environment, chunky charming stylized stone shapes, confident dark outlines, clean cel shaded color blocks, deliberately limited cool palette and simplified textures, compatible with small chibi pixel sprites. Not realistic, not a 3D render, not intricate painterly concept art. Scene: ancient spiritual stone gate amid ruined arches, blue midnight forest and distant ruined towers, a glowing cyan circular magic portal, tiny blue spirit flames, low curling stylized mist. Preserve dark mystical fantasy essence while making the visual approachable, graphic and readable. Composition for a centered login form: keep the central 40 percent relatively dark, calm and uncluttered with broad stone pathway and mist, frame it with chunky broken stone columns on left and right; luminous portal sits in distant background above and just to the right of center, avoid overpowering light. Foreground has simple broken paving stones and small silhouettes of plants; layered parallax-like 2D depth. Palette navy, indigo, muted teal, cold cyan highlights. Crisp hand-drawn cartoon contours and flat shadow shapes. Restrained luminous accents. No characters, no text, no logo, no UI, no buttons, no panel frames, no watermark. Fill the entire landscape canvas with one coherent scene.
